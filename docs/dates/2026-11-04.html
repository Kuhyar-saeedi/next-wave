What the professor asks

From the project brief.

  • Step 5: Choose methods because they perform a task: explore, open the agenda, deliberate, generate ideas, produce knowledge, test and adapt.
  • Step 6: For every phase, on-site, online or both? Synchronous and asynchronous moments. How information moves between settings. Digital tools for continuity, not to copy meetings. Alternatives for low digital skills.
  • Journey Map: before, on-site, online and after, with the connections between moments.

Study guide

In one minute

Every method does a job. We explore (doorstep and walking interviews, focus groups, mapping), open the agenda (Open Space day), deliberate (fairness assembly chosen by lottery, scenarios, fishbowl), generate ideas (World Café, co-design workshops), produce knowledge (citizen science) and test (living lab, usability tests). The hybrid design keeps one process across the courtyard and the screen.

Key points

  • Three method choices: not survey-only; vulnerable groups first in their own spaces; a lottery for the binding decision.
  • Four hybrid rules: every online step has an offline twin; meet to decide, go online to continue; information flows both ways within 48 hours; no smartphone needed.
  • Six channels: Energy Corner noticeboard, energy ambassadors, printed bulletin, WhatsApp per language, web platform, phone line.
  • Online access is not inclusion: printed "sunny hours" calendar, paper simulator, help desk, paper votes count the same.
  • The Journey Map shows one tenant's path: before, on-site, online, after.

Practice questions

Why did you choose these methods?
Each performs a task in a chain: listening first, then tenants set the agenda, then deliberation on the hard choice, then knowledge and testing. REGACE (Article 1) combined the same three methods (Open Space, World Café, fishbowl); we reuse them, each placed where its job is needed.
How do on-site and online connect?
Online comments are read aloud at the next session; on-site results go online within 48 hours; ambassadors digitise paper postcards; a monthly "you said, we did" log closes the loop.
