Study guide

In one minute

"Fair sharing" is abstract: tenants cannot vote on a formula they cannot see working. The Fair Share Simulator shows, family by family, what each rule would mean in euros. Pick a sunny or cloudy month, mix the rules with sliders, and see each household's share change live, with a plain-words explanation.

The four rules

Rule Idea Trade-off
Equal Same amount for every household Simple, ignores need and effort
Need More for low-income or larger households Fights energy poverty, needs sensitive data
Effort More for using energy in sunny hours Raises the bonus, penalises shift workers
Floor A minimum for everyone, whatever the mix Protects those who cannot shift

Practice questions

What would you learn by testing it?
Which rule tenants prefer and whether that changes once they see the effect on others; whether the algorithm is explainable; what data tenants accept to share; whether paper works as well as screen.
