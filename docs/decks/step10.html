3 / 6

The before / during / after test

Documenting the trace

BEFORE

Freeze the developers' starting design: incentive-maximising AI, equal split, full automation, app only, provider holds the data.

Signed and dated.

DURING

Record every proposal, data finding, controversy, prototype test and rejected alternative in the change log.

AFTER

Compare the final design with the frozen one, line by line, and trace each change back to a contribution.

What would count as evidence

Participation worked if…

At least one design element changed and can be traced to tenants' input

The fairness assembly mirrored the complex, not only the usual participants

Vulnerable households' share was not lower than under the developers' default

Tenants can explain the rule that shares their money

A tenant group keeps monitoring after the pilot ends

6 / 6

The unresolved dilemma

Fairness versus a bigger bonus

The tension

Rewarding effort raises the community's total income. Protecting those who can't shift lowers it. Tenants may choose a fairer but smaller pie.

The legal limit

Tenants without a regular contract may not be able to join the CER. Participation can include them in the discussion, but not, alone, in the benefits.

We accept both as open questions, and our process makes them visible instead of hiding them in code.
