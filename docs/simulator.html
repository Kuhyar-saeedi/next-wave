What data does this mix need?

Each rule needs different information about households. Tenants decide this in the data charter (Gate 3).

  • Nothing personalEqual and Floor need only the list of members.
  • Household size and incomeNeeded by the Need rule. Sensitive: who sees it, and how is it checked?
  • Hourly meter dataNeeded by the Effort rule. Shows when people are at home.

How the numbers are made

Every figure is an illustration for discussion, not a forecast for a real building.

  • Incentive. Shared energy earns a premium tariff of €80/MWh plus a variable part that rises when market prices fall, capped at €120/MWh for plants up to 200 kW, paid for 20 years (DM 414/2023). Lazio adds €4/MWh, and ARERA returns about €8/MWh. Sources: Assolombarda, CCIAA Verona.
  • Solar production. About 1,500 kWh per kWp per year, spread by month and over daylight hours. This is a typical value for a south-facing roof in Rome, not a measurement.
  • Households. 60 homes: ten of each of the six fictional types. Their daily use and hourly patterns are invented to be plausible; the citizen science strand (Step 7) would replace them with real diaries.
  • Rules. The Floor is paid first. The rest is split by the mix: Equal per household; Need by points (one per person, two more for low income); Effort by each home's part of the shared energy, hour by hour.
  • Households still pay their normal bills. This is the extra money the community earns. The rules also require surplus beyond a threshold to go to consumers or social purposes, which a tenant-written rule respects.
