The paper at a glance

How do farmers see agrivoltaics, and can they help shape it?

Research question

How do farming communities perceive agrivoltaics, and which social, economic and practical factors shape acceptance?

Who and where

The sociological work package (WP6) of the EU project REGACE, with farmers in Italy, Austria, Germany, Israel and Greece.

Method

Mixed methods: 44 in-depth interviews, then participatory sessions combining Open Space, World Café and a fishbowl.

Core argument

A disruptive technology only works if end users take part in shaping it from the first stages, not just at the end.

Next Wave · Article 1 — Antonucci et al. (2025)

The authors frame agrivoltaics with Bower and Christensen's theory of disruptive innovation (1995): new technologies can transform a sector, but only if the people who must use them accept and understand them.
