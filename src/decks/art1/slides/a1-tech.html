The technology and the gap

Solar panels and crops on the same land

What REGACE builds

Next-generation agrivoltaic greenhouses: photovoltaic panels combined with farming, plus CO2 enrichment for higher yields.

The promise: energy security and food security from the same land.

What research was missing

Most studies measure technical performance. Farmers' perceptions, concerns and needs were mostly ignored.

The authors call this a major barrier to adoption.
