Three contributory cases

Simple data collection, real impact

FLANDERS · 2018

CuriousNoses

20,000 people measured NO2 air pollution in their street for a month.

Impact: air quality became an election issue; a Brussels court accepted the data as indicative evidence; people changed how they commute.

UGANDA · 2019

ATRAP

25 trained volunteers reported snails that spread parasitic diseases, weekly, by phone app.

Impact: warning signs at risky sites; volunteers called "Snail Doctor"; one entered local politics; copied in Chad.

NEW ZEALAND · 2016–

Flip the Fleet

645 electric-car drivers share monthly data on distance, charging and efficiency.

Impact: better-informed buyers and a public debate on EV policy; it is slowly moving toward co-creation.
