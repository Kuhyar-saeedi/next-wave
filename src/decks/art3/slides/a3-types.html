Three meanings of citizen science

From data gatherers to co-researchers

Contributory

Volunteers collect data; experts design and interpret. Tradition of amateur science (Bonney).

Participatory

Science opened up to wider publics and done by citizens themselves (Irwin, 1995).

Co-creative

Citizens take part in every stage, from the research question to the analysis (Shirk et al., 2012).

The critiques

Unpaid labour serving science or industry; experiential knowledge ignored; and in the US, participants are mostly white, above-median income, college-educated.
