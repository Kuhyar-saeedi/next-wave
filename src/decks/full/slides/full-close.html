The answer to the course question

What will be different because people participated?

The rule

The money would be shared by a fairness rule tenants chose and voted on, not one written by the provider.

The data

The AI would learn from tenant-collected knowledge about real daily life, under data rules tenants agreed.

The access

Elders, shift workers and non-Italian speakers could use and control the assistant without a smartphone.

Participation did not decorate the technology. It is designed to change it.
