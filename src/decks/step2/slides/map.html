Centre: the innovation. Inner ring: the tenant groups, all vulnerable, who live with the system. Middle ring: those who make it happen. Outside: rules, money and the wider context. Dark = high influence, white = lower influence. Key links: ATER and the committee conflict over trust and maintenance; tenants and the provider conflict over privacy and automation; knowledge flows from tenants to researchers to the provider, and meter data from Areti to the provider; the CER depends on ATER's roofs and GSE's incentive; the committee is allied with unions and charities, the City with ATER.
