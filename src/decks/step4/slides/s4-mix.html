Why not co-create everything?

Wide for everyone, deep for volunteers

Wide track

Every household can be informed, consulted and vote at the gates, with little time and no smartphone.

Deep track

A rotating group of volunteers co-designs and co-creates, with childcare, flexible times and recognition.

As Article 3 argues, not everyone has the time to co-create, but everyone should be able to decide at the gates.

Next Wave · Step 4 — Levels of participation

The deep track rotates so that it does not become a closed group of the usual participants. Its proposals always go back to the wide track for a decision.
