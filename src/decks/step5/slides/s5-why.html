Why these methods

Three design choices

Not survey-only

A survey can't explain why a family cooks at 9 pm. Interviews, diaries and dialogue can.

Separate, then together

Vulnerable groups speak first in their own spaces, then meet the others with their points ready.

Lottery, not volunteers

For the binding fairness decision, a mirror of the complex beats the loudest residents.
