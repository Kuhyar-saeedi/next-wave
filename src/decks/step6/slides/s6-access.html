Online access is not inclusion

Alternatives for low digital skills

Assistant without an app

Weekly printed "sunny hours" calendar, SMS or a phone call instead of notifications.

Simulator on paper

Printable household cards and coins to play the fairness rules at the table.

Digital help desk

Ambassadors help on request, building digital skills instead of replacing them.

Equal weight

A paper vote counts exactly like an online one, and is counted in public.
