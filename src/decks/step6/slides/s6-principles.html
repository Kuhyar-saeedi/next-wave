Four design rules

Digital for continuity, not to copy meetings

1

Every online step has an offline twin

Each vote, comment or survey also works on paper, by phone or in person.

2

Meet to decide, go online to continue

Decisions happen face to face; online keeps the thread between meetings.

3

Information flows both ways

Online comments are read aloud on site; on-site results are posted online within 48 hours.

4

No smartphone needed

Full participation is possible without any device, as Article 2 warns about digital capital.
