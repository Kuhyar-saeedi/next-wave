The six-part design

From question to change

PartOur answer
QuestionRQ1–RQ3: routines, forecast errors, effects of fairness rules
ProtocolCo-written in a workshop by tenants and researchers; tested with 5 households first
ParticipationPostcards for all; smart plugs, diaries and shade photos for volunteers
ValidationDiaries checked against meter data; training; uncertainty shown openly
InterpretationMonthly data evenings where tenants and researchers read results together
FeedbackFindings change the model, the messages and the fairness rule, logged publicly

The professor's six-step model: question, protocol, participation, validation, interpretation, feedback. The key point is that citizens are present in the protocol and the interpretation, not only in data collection.
