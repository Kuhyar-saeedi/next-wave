Avoiding the extractive model

Tenants are not unpaid sensors

Data belongs to the CER

The provider only processes it, under the tenants' data charter.

Privacy by design

Aggregated by default, deleted after the pilot, GDPR consent per use.

Opt out, no penalty

Not sharing data never reduces a household's share.

Results returned

Each household gets its own "energy postcard" in plain language.

Right to challenge

Any finding can be contested at a data evening and re-checked.

Recognition

Named credit, certificates and a small allowance for volunteers.

Next Wave · Step 7 — Citizen science

These six points answer the professor's slide "avoid the extractive model": data ownership, privacy and future use, recognition of local knowledge, returning results in understandable forms, and a mechanism to challenge findings.
