2 · Accessibility

Remove the barriers before people meet them

Language

Plain Italian, key materials translated, mediators at sessions

Timing

Each activity offered twice: weekday evening and Saturday morning

Mobility

Ground-floor rooms in the complex; home visits if needed

Disability

Large print, audio versions, accessible routes and seating

Digital access

Every step works on paper, phone or in person (Step 6)

Care and cost

Childcare, snacks, and a small voucher for long sessions
