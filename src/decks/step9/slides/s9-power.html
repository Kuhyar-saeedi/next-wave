3 · Power balance

Experts on tap, not on top

Neutral facilitators

Not from ATER or the provider; trained in conflict and inclusion.

Tenants speak first

Experts answer questions; they don't open discussions.

No-jargon rule

Anyone can raise a "say it simply" card at any time.

Tenants hold the vote

On fairness and data, only tenants vote; institutions give opinions.
