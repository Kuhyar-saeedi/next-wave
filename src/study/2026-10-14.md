## In one minute

Families in Rome's public housing struggle with energy bills. Our AI energy assistant for a solar energy community (CER) does three things: it **predicts** the roof's solar production, **advises** families when to run appliances, and **shares the money** using a fairness rule the tenants decide together. The third function is what makes it new, and the reason participation is necessary.

## Step 1: six answers to know

| Question | Short answer |
| --- | --- |
| Technology and problem | AI assistant for a solar CER: forecast, advise, share money fairly |
| Who develops or promotes it | New idea; builds on solar forecasting and smart plugs. EU, state, GSE, City of Rome support CERs; ATER owns the buildings |
| Where | A public housing complex in Rome's eastern periphery, e.g. Tor Bella Monaca |
| Benefits | Lower costs, fair sharing, energy know-how, cleaner energy |
| Risks | What is fair? Privacy, trust, exclusion, costs |
| Why participation | The AI decides who gets money, so tenants must help write its rules |

## Step 2: what the map shows

- **Closest, but weakest:** tenants are at the centre of the innovation but hold the least power over resources, data and decisions.
- **Data is the conflict:** the provider and the grid operator (Areti) want data; tenants fear losing privacy and control.
- **Trusted bridges:** the tenant committee, unions (SUNIA, Unione Inquilini), charities and the parish can reach voices institutions cannot.
- **Vulnerable groups:** elderly people living alone, low-income and migrant families, shift workers, people with disabilities, tenants without a contract.

## Practice questions

### Q: Who defined the problem?
So far, we did, from the developers' side. That is why Step 3 rewrites it with the tenants' voices, and why the first gate lets tenants set the agenda.

### Q: Who is missing?
Women carers, young people, shift workers, migrants, irregular tenants and neighbours outside the CER. Each one has a reason for being absent and a reason it matters (Step 2, missing voices slide).
