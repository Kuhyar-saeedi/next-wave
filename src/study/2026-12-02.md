## In one minute

We go to people instead of waiting for them, through trusted bridges. Every activity runs twice (weekday evening and Saturday morning), in accessible rooms, with translations, childcare and a phone line. Experts are "on tap, not on top": neutral facilitators, tenants speak first, a "say it simply" card, and only tenants vote on fairness and data.

## Key points

- **Conflict:** name it in a public log, explore it in a fishbowl, test it with data, decide it at the gate with minority views attached.
- **If tenants disagree with developers:** the gates bind; the provider adapts or the pilot pauses; an external mediator is called before any override.
- **Traceability:** every proposal gets a number and a public status.
- **Feedback:** results within 48 hours, a monthly bulletin and courtyard event, an energy postcard per household.

## Practice questions

### Q: How do you stop dominant actors monopolising the discussion?
Neutral facilitators evaluated by participants, tenants speak before experts, a no-jargon card, and tenant-only votes on fairness and data.

### Q: What happens if stakeholders disagree with the developers?
The gate decision binds. The provider adapts or the pilot pauses, and the university mediates before any override.
