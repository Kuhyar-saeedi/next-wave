## Step 10 in one minute

We judge the process by what changed in the technology, not by headcount. Six dimensions: inclusion, dialogue, knowledge, learning, innovation, continuity. **Before:** freeze the developers' design, signed and dated. **During:** log every proposal, finding and controversy. **After:** compare line by line and trace each change to a contribution.

## The core question

> What will be different because people participated?

The fairness rule, the data ownership, the advise-or-automate choice, the channels, the AI's household types and the governance could all change, and we record why each did or did not.

## Practice questions

### Q: What would count as evidence that participation worked?
At least one design element changed and is traced to tenants; the assembly mirrored the complex; vulnerable households were not worse off than under the default; tenants can explain the rule; a group keeps monitoring.

### Q: What is your unresolved dilemma?
Rewarding effort raises total income, protecting those who cannot shift lowers it: tenants may choose a fairer but smaller pie. And tenants without a contract may not be able to join the CER at all.
